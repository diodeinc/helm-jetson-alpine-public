# Helm P3767-0001 bring-up record

Validated on 2026-08-13 and 2026-08-14 with Diode Helm carriers and Jetson
Orin NX 8 GB modules (P3767-0001).

## Software and boot

- Alpine Linux 3.24.1
- NVIDIA Jetson Linux BSP R39.2
- Linux `6.8.12-1021-tegra`
- Native macOS RCM recovery, QSPI installation, NVMe installation, and cold
  boot completed successfully
- Live model: `Diode Helm with NVIDIA Jetson Orin NX/Nano`
- Root filesystem: `/dev/nvme0n1p1`; FAT ESP: `/dev/nvme0n1p2`
- QSPI image SHA-256:
  `c14fc142ef2c8f19b3c9f2f6f6a5dec596f84e6a7e8d45a1f520027ee97f2f8b`
- Live QSPI MTD geometry: 67,108,864-byte device with a 4,096-byte erase
  unit. NVIDIA's 65,536-byte firmware-layout stride is retained separately in
  the image catalog.

## Recovery-USB-only provisioning

On 2026-08-14, a second P3767-0001 Helm was provisioned from macOS with only
power and the USB2 recovery connector attached; the onboard MCP2221 UART was
absent. The qualified RCM blob was 167,035,095 bytes. It RAM-booted, switched
the recovery port to device mode, and enumerated on macOS as the exact guarded
identity `0955:7020`, product `Helm Alpine Recovery Console`, serial
`helm-recovery`.

The target then completed, in one host command:

- module EEPROM match for `699-13767-0001-300`;
- PNY CS2142 500 GB NVMe geometry and payload preflight;
- 64 MiB QSPI geometry and image checksum preflight;
- GPT, ext4 `APP`, FAT ESP, Alpine, DTB, and fallback UEFI installation;
- complete factory-QSPI backup to the new NVMe, with SHA-256
  `0c3a539e384659ecb2412900ca128578bbd34364e9929f6134100551cb8d6dbd`;
- complete QSPI erase/write and full readback verification against image
  SHA-256
  `c14fc142ef2c8f19b3c9f2f6f6a5dec596f84e6a7e8d45a1f520027ee97f2f8b`;
- sync, unmount, and a session-bound success marker accepted by the Mac.

A separate live host-side selector check, performed while both USB devices
were visible, verified that the MCP2221 UART cannot be mistaken for recovery
CDC. Recovery now includes and loads `pwm-tegra` followed by `pwm-fan`, then
requests full fan PWM during provisioning.

The post-provision cold boot on this second unit was not independently checked
over Ethernet because the Mac's ASIX adapter itself disconnected. The cold
boot and runtime measurements below were completed on the first P3767-0001
unit.

## Interface results

| Interface | Negotiated link | Read/throughput result | Method |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ethernet, RTL8111/8168 | 1 Gb/s full duplex | 942 Mb/s Mac→Helm; 942 Mb/s Helm→Mac | `iperf3`, four streams, 10 seconds |
| USB1/J6, USBSS lane 1 | USB 3.2 Gen 2, 10 Gb/s (`usb 2-2`) | 992.5 MB/s | 2 GiB direct read from an unmounted SHARGE disk |
| USB2/J7, USBSS lane 2 | USB 3.2 Gen 2, 10 Gb/s (`usb 2-3`) | 995.4 MB/s | 2 GiB direct read from the same unmounted disk |
| NVMe, PNY CS2142 500 GB | PCIe Gen4 x4, 16 GT/s x4 | 4.9 GB/s | 2 GiB direct read |

The USB storage checks were read-only and did not mount or modify the SHARGE
disk. Recovery/J9 is intentionally USB 2.0-only; SuperSpeed is routed only to
USB1/J6 and USB2/J7.

Representative read command:

```sh
dd if=/dev/sda of=/dev/null bs=16M count=128 iflag=direct
```

## Carrier peripherals

- Both Helm GPIO LEDs were discovered. The chase/flash demo completed and
  restored green heartbeat plus orange disk-activity triggers.
- The fan ran at full PWM during the initial benchmarks. After the thermal
  governor settled at cooling state 1/3, its tachometer reported 1,796 RPM at
  PWM 88/255.
- Observed temperature during sustained I/O was approximately 52–55 °C; the
  final hottest-sensor reading was 53.9 °C.
- The XUSB controller failed one runtime resume after a hot unplug with Falcon
  state `0xffffffff`. Keeping only the XUSB controller's runtime-PM policy at
  `on` prevented recurrence while moving the same disk between both ports.

## Qualification boundary

The P3767-0001 path has physical boot, storage, Ethernet, USB, PCIe, fan, and
LED coverage. The P3767-0000, 0003, 0004, and 0005 artifacts pass native build,
catalog, QSPI-image, and RCM-bundle verification but still require equivalent
testing on their exact modules.
